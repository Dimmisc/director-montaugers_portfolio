Off Camera.

I’m a film director, editor and colorist. My work spans narrative films, music videos, television and commercial content. I worked at ARTIFACT PRODUCTION and filmed and reported for Public Television of Russia (OTR).

I’m drawn to projects that build a world around a product, artist or brand. I develop the concept and script, then carry the visual idea through the shoot, edit and final grade.

Services

Directing & Creative Development

Get a concept, script and visual direction your team can agree on before the shoot.

Video Production

Get footage that covers the scenes and shots your edit needs.

Editing & Postproduction

Turn footage into a cut with a clear story and rhythm. Add motion graphics or VFX where the brief calls for them.

Color Grading

Give your film a consistent look across shots, with color that supports its mood and directs attention.
